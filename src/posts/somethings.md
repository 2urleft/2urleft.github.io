## geometry

\[2\] in vietnam, you're supposed to learn this in 9th grade, but in my school you're going to learn this in 10th grade. here are some famous identities:
$$\sin \alpha = \frac{O}{H}; \cos \alpha = \frac{A}{H}; \tan \alpha = \frac{O}{A}$$
$$\csc \alpha = \frac{1}{\sin \alpha}; \sec \alpha = \frac{1}{\cos \alpha}; \cot \alpha = \frac{1}{\tan \alpha}$$
$$\sin^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \alpha = 1$$
$$1 + \tan^2 \alpha = \frac{1}{\cos^2 \alpha}$$

if $\alpha + \beta = 90^\circ$:
$$\sin \alpha = \cos \beta$$
$$\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{\tan \beta}$$

## algebra

\[5\] hệ thức viète is a simultaneous for all polynomials that describes the addition and product of roots. the one learnt in 9th grade is for the quadratic:
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1+x_2=-\frac{b}{a} \\\\
x_1.x_2 = \frac{c}{a}
\end{cases}
$$
also as a result:
$$x_1^2+x_2^2=(x_1+x_2)^2 - 2 x_1 x_2$$

\[6\] for "exceptional" students in math, they also learn the AM-GM inequality:
$$\frac{x_1+...+x_n}{n} \ge \sqrt[n]{x_1...x_n}$$
and cauchy-schwarz:
$$(x_1^2+x_2^2+...)(y_1^2+y_2^2+...)\ge (x_1y_1+x_2y_2+...)^2$$
$$\frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2} + ... \frac{1}{x_n} \ge \frac{n^2}{a_1+a_2+...+a_n}$$
